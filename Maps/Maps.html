Maps are the locations that players will experience Guts & Blackpowder in, there are two types of maps: campaign and endless. All maps involve fighting zombies. Campaign maps follow a story-driven or progression based route and have an end to them, with unique interactions and objectives. completing these maps awards the player francs or an achievement. Endless maps are the traditional style of zombie maps, infinitely spawning zombies in a wave-based format. The amount of zombies and their health will get higher and higher as the rounds increases, every few rounds the player will be awarded francs, the map ends when all the survivors are consumed by the horde or dies. The maps are NOT listed in chronological order.

Campaign maps

Vardohus Fortress

50 Francs (Survival, one life)
70 Francs (Hardcore, survival, one life)
+ awarded francs (crucifix, healing, etc...)

Located in Vardø, Norway, players must progress through a Danish fortress, and then a town, before ultimately reaching a small port, where the survivors must climb a lighthouse and turn it on, attracting a nearby British warship, the Undaunted. Players must hold out at 2 of the final destinations on the map, there are no respawns on the map itself, though dead players will respawn as Royal Navy Marines when the Undaunted arrives. The map has a freezing mechanic, but is less noticeable due to the many heat sources around the major areas of the map.

Copenhagen

125 Francs (Survival, one life)
80 Francs (Survival, respawned) / (Hardcore, survival, respawned)
150 Francs (Hardcore, survival, one life)
+ awarded francs (crucifix, healing, etc...)

Located in the place of the same name, also the capital of Norway, a very long map. The map takes place in some crowded spaces and buildings, combined with the bomber spawns these can be very dangerous, take care to ensure that bombers are killed a safe distance away from players. Tthe bulk of the difficulty comes from the length and occasional human error of one's team mates. Players must pilot a tiny boat to fight the British ship enforcing the blockade of Copenhagen at the end. Line infantry and sappers are going to be the best options for this map, line infantry have accurate weapons that can deal with the NPCs that man the British vessel by firing at them. The line infantry's pike can also be great for holding down the zombie hordes on the objectives, while sappers allow players more safety when completing objectives by building barricades or preparing the ones on the boat to block cannon and gun fire.

San Sebastian

100 Francs (Survival, one life)
100 Francs (Sacrifice)
70 Francs (Survival, respawned)
125 Francs (Hardcore, survival, one life)
80 Francs (Hardcore, respawned)
+ awarded francs (crucifix, healing, etc...)

One of the longer objective maps, takes a while to complete. Set during the Siege of San Sebastian. The map has alot of zombies, if uncareful they can easily dwindle down the team. The holdout sections are fairly difficult aswell without proper coordination and sappers. After Barry dies, a player will have to climb up to lower the winch on the last part, they will be awarded francs for doing so. Sappers and surgeons are good due to the map's length, ranged units are almost required to hold ground.

Leipzig

80 Francs (Survival, one life)
60 Francs (Survival, respawned)
100 Francs (Hardcore, survival, one life)
70 Francs (Hardcore, respawned)
+ awarded francs (crucifix, healing, etc...)

One of the largest battles of the Napoleonic Wars, the map features the most nations. The map is generally alright in difficulty, the spawn could take out a few players, and the cart objectives could slowly dwindle down the survivors. The amount of zombies that spawn could also surround players, but if the team moves quickly this should be no issue. The map ends with a swift dash to the other side of the White Elster river before the bridge explodes. Offsensive units are most beneficial on this map due to the map's constant spawning of zombies and the moving objectives, though sappers can be good for holding back zombies at the last objective, or for spawn camping at the gunpowder cart area.

Kaub

100 Francs (Survival, one life)
70 Francs (Survival, respawned)
125 Francs (Hardcore, survival, one life)
80 Francs (Hardcore, respawned)
+ awarded francs (crucifix, healing, etc...)

Located in the town of Kaub, and the Pfalzgrafenstein Castle along the Rhine river, takes place during general Blucher's crossing of the Rhine. Likely the hardest objective map in the game, many holdout sections with infinitely spawning zombies with punishing and dangerous bomber spawns; featuring many hard sections with many runners, zappers, and bombers. The sheer difficulty of zombies alone on this map calls for sappers, who can help reduce the stress and keep zombies back while the team completes the objective. Firearms are also necessary to reduce ground lost at the holdout sections, and to prevent further damage to other players and/or buildings via igniters or bombers. The map introduces some fetch tasks, where players have to take items to a carriage, each item carried may slow the player down until they place it on the carriage.

Catacombes de Paris

80 Francs (Survival, one life)
60 Francs (Survival, respawned)
100 Francs (Hardcore, survival, one life)
70 Francs (Hardcore, respawned)
+ awarded francs (crucifix, healing, etc...)

As per the name, the map takes place in the underground catacombs of Paris (initially), when the undead horde has conquered Paris. Players will have to advance through the catacombs to detour through to central Paris, where players will then have to hold off the zombie horde until a ferryman arrives. The catacombs section isn't difficult, the final holdout at the bridge isn't either. Due to how open the final holdout is, alongside how open the sightlines are, ranged classes are recommended due to their melee offensive capabilities and their abilities to take care of threats like bombers and igniters from afar. Players will also have access to 2 cannons situated at the back, making defending the bridge relatively easy if there are many offensive units.

Berezina

~ 200 Francs (Survival, earnings are related to contribution)
+ awarded francs (crucifix, healing, etc...)

A holdout map, relatively easy difficulty if most players follow the same pattern each respawn/wave, the final bridge crossing could be slightly dangerous. Takes place around Napoleon's retreat from Russia, across the Berezina river. Players will occasionally respawn when a carriage that comes every so often arrives, the map goes on forever until players all die OR build the bridge and cross. Players can pick up a claw hammer near the base of the bridge if they are not a sapper, which will be used over the wooden logs that are placed on the bridge to hammer it down. The carriage will drop off wood, which players can then carry to place down on a highlighted section of the bridge or drop it off somewhere else. Once the bridge is built, horses will cross, and a random section of the bridge will break, once that section is repaired, players may get on the back of the supply carriage once it crosses to finish the map and survive. Uniquely, this is the only map where deaths don't matter for completion. All classes are effective.

Endless maps

(Franc earnings can be found in the endless mode page, click to be linked there for the page)

Hougomont

A traditional endless map, takes place historically in the farm of Hougomont during the Battle of Waterloo. Considered one of the easier endless maps, it's very open, which could benefit kiting in some ways. The map allows for various tactics, but sharpshooters are the most beneficial on this map. Hordes are typically really large, but with the correct team they can be easily taken care of.

La Ferme d'En-haut

Endless map that takes place prior to the Battle of Waterloo, at Ligny. Historically, the French were flanked by the Prussians at Ligny. The map features cannons, if used properly they could have devastating results. However, the design of the map and the zombie spawns will likely result in the players being cornered. Emphasises the importance of sappers, can achieve higher-wave runs if played well.

Tyrolean Village

A previously peaceful village in Tyrol, Bavaria. The map has multiple zombie spawnpoints all converging on an intersection, there are many buildings in the map where players can hold out or shoot at zombies. The map emphasises both sappers and offensive units, due to the map's crowded design.

La Haye Sainte

A walled farmhouse not far off from Hougomont, another crucial site during the Battle of Waterloo. The map is hard to clutch due to its small and cramped design, alongside the zombie spawns. The barn is the common place to hold off zombies on this map, due to the ladder that zombies have to climb, pikes and swords can be extremely effective at dispatching zombies. Recommended to have a few ranged units to take care of igniters and bombers that may make entry into the barn.
