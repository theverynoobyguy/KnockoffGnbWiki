Maps are the locations that players will experience Guts & Blackpowder in, there are two types of maps: campaign and endless. All maps involve fighting zombies. Campaign maps follow a story-driven or progression based route and have an end to them, with unique interactions and objectives. completing these maps awards the player francs or an achievement. Endless maps are the traditional style of zombie maps, infinitely spawning zombies in a wave-based format. The amount of zombies and their health will get higher and higher as the rounds increases, every few rounds the player will be awarded francs, the map ends when all the survivors are consumed by the horde or dies.

Endless maps

Hougomont

A traditional endless map, takes place historically in the farm of Hougomont during the Battle of Waterloo. Considered one of the easier endless maps, it's very open, which could benefit kiting in some ways. The map allows for various tactics, but sharpshooters are the most beneficial on this map. Hordes are typically really large, but with the correct team they can be easily taken care of.

La Ferme d'En-haut

Endless map that takes place prior to the Battle of Waterloo, at Ligny. Historically, the French were flanked by the Prussians at Ligny. The map features cannons, if used properly they could have devastating results. However, the design of the map and the zombie spawns will likely result in the players being cornered. Emphasises the importance of sappers, can achieve higher-wave runs if played well.

Tyrolean Village

A previously peaceful village in Tyrol, Bavaria. The map has multiple zombie spawnpoints all converging on an intersection, there are many buildings in the map where players can hold out or shoot at zombies. The map emphasises both sappers and offensive units, due to the map's crowded design.

La Haye Sainte

A walled farmhouse not far off from Hougomont, another crucial site during the Battle of Waterloo. The map is hard to clutch due to its small and cramped design, alongside the zombie spawns. The barn is the main site to hold off zombies on this map, due to the ladder that zombies have to climb, pikes and swords can be extremely effective at clearing zombies.
