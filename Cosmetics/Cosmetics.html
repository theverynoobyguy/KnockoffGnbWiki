Cosmetic items in Guts & Blackpowder can be different regiments, blemishes, and accessories that can change the appearance of the player. While some come for free, others are obtained from unlocking achievements or by directly purchasing them through their respective accessory packs. Regiments are exclusive to only a certain nation, the player can only play in that regiment's uniform if their respective nation is present on the map they are playing. Accessories and blemishes are universal, and can always change the appearance of the player, no matter what nation their character belongs to.
